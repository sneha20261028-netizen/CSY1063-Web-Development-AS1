01

Climate Clarity

Climate Clarity is an AI-powered climate information platform designed to make complex climate reports easier for people in Nepal to understand and use. The platform addresses the problem of technical climate information being difficult for ordinary citizens to access and understand.

Climate Clarity uses AI to transform lengthy climate reports into simple visual summaries and understandable insights. It provides information at a local level through an interactive map covering all 77 districts of Nepal, helping users explore climate-related information relevant to their communities.

Key Features

  • AI-powered climate report summarisation
  • Interactive map covering Nepal's 77 districts
  • Climate transparency scorecards
  • Bilingual voice audio explanations
  • Climate promise and commitment tracker
  • Simple visual summaries of complex climate data

The main goal of Climate Clarity is to make climate information more accessible, transparent, and useful for everyone. By converting complicated reports into clear summaries, maps, visual data, and audio explanations, the platform helps citizens understand climate risks and become more informed about climate action in Nepal.

HTML · CSS · AI

03

RescueMS-Zoo Management System

RescueMS is a web-based Zoo Management System designed to improve the management of animals, visitors, memberships, sponsorships, events, and other zoo activities. The system provides a central platform where zoo staff can manage important information efficiently while visitors can access services and information more easily.

The project focuses on creating an organised and user-friendly digital experience for both zoo administrators and visitors. It includes different features for managing animal records, memberships, sponsorships, bookings, events, and educational content.

Key Features

  • Animal management and animal records
  • Membership registration and renewal
  • Animal sponsorship programmes
  • Visitor booking and event management
  • Admin login and role-based access
  • Virtual zoo tour
  • Educational learning resources
  • Visitor-friendly information and services

The main goal of RescueMS is to make zoo management more organised, efficient, and accessible. By bringing different zoo services into one digital system, the project can help staff manage information effectively while providing visitors with a better online experience.

Project Role: Interface Design

Technologies and Concepts: UI/UX Design, Web Development, System Analysis, Database Design, and Role-Based Access.

Figma · Database · System Design

Aura Study

Aura Study is a digital learning platform designed to provide students with a simple and engaging environment for studying, accessing learning resources, and improving their academic experience. The project focuses on creating an organised and user-friendly interface where students can easily find and use educational content.

The main purpose of Aura Study is to make learning more accessible through a clean digital platform. It demonstrates how web technology can be used to organise educational information and create a more convenient learning experience for students.

Key Features

  • Student-friendly learning interface
  • Organised educational resources
  • Simple and easy navigation
  • Accessible study materials
  • Clean and responsive user interface
  • Designed to support independent learning

Through this project, I explored how user interface design and web development can be combined to create a useful educational platform. Aura Study also helped me improve my understanding of designing websites with students and usability in mind.

Technologies Used: HTML5, CSS3 and Web Development.
