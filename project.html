    AuraStudy-AI Study Assistant

    AuraStudy is an AI-powered study planning and productivity platform designed to help students organise their learning and manage their study time more effectively. The platform provides a personalised **Study Planner** where students can schedule lectures, revision sessions, assignments, and other academic tasks in a weekly or monthly calendar.

    The system also provides **Smart Insights** to analyse a student's performance and suggest ways to improve their study schedule. By combining study planning, task management, and AI-based recommendations, AuraStudy aims to make learning more organised, personalised, and productive.

    Key Features

    * AI Study Optimisation-helps create and improve study schedules. * Study Planner-organise lectures, revision sessions, and other activities. * Task Management - manage assignments, reading tasks, and academic deadlines. * Smart Insights-provides personalised suggestions based on study performance. * Flashcards- support quick revision and active learning. * Smart Notes-organise important study materials and notes. * Study Progress-helps students monitor their academic activities. * Task Reminders-keeps students aware of upcoming academic deadlines. * Personalised Learning- creates a study experience based on individual needs.

    What I Learned

    Working on AuraStudy helped me understand how AI can be combined with web-based applications to solve real student problems. I developed my understanding of **user interface design, study planning systems, AI-based recommendations, task management, information organisation, and user experience. The project also helped me think about how technology can make learning more structured, accessible, and personalised for students.

    Climate Clarity

    Climate Clarity is an AI-powered climate information platform created to make complex climate information easier to understand and access for people across Nepal. The project focuses on Digitisation for Climate Change by transforming lengthy and technical climate reports into simple, meaningful information.

    The platform uses AI to convert large climate reports into visual summaries, interactive district-level information, transparency scorecards, bilingual audio explanations, and a promise tracker. These features help users understand climate-related issues without needing specialist knowledge. The project also focuses on making climate information more accessible to people from different backgrounds and locations.

    Climate Clarity was developed as a team project, where I contributed to the development and design of the project. The experience helped me improve my skills in research, UI/UX design, teamwork, problem-solving, and presenting technical information in a user-friendly way.

    Key Features:

  • AI-powered climate report summarisation
  • Information covering all 77 districts of Nepal
  • Interactive climate information
  • Climate transparency scorecards
  • Bilingual audio explanations
  • Climate commitment and promise tracker
  • Simple and accessible presentation of complex data

    RescueMS

    RescueMS (Rescue Management System) is a web-based zoo management system designed to improve the management of animals, visitors, memberships, sponsorships, events, and administrative activities. The system provides a centralised platform for zoo staff while also giving visitors an easy way to interact with zoo services.

    Visitors can explore animal information, learn about conservation, view upcoming events, make bookings, become members, and participate in animal sponsorship programmes. Staff and administrators can manage animal records, visitor information, events, memberships, and other important zoo activities through the system.

    The main aim of RescueMS is to create a simple, organised, and user-friendly digital experience while improving the efficiency of zoo management. Working on this project helped me develop my understanding of system analysis, interface design, database concepts, user requirements, teamwork, and web development.

    Key Features

    • Animal management and information
    • Visitor booking system
    • Membership management
    • Animal sponsorship programme
    • Event management
    • Educational and conservation information
    • Administrative management features
    • User-friendly and responsive interface

    What I Learned

    Working on this project helped me connect technical knowledge with real-world problems. I developed stronger skills in designing user interfaces, organising information, researching user needs, working as part of a team, and creating digital solutions that are useful and accessible to users.
