Featured Project

RescueMS

RescueMS (Rescue Management System) is a web-based zoo management system designed to improve the way a zoo manages animals, visitors, memberships, sponsorships, events, and administrative activities. The system provides a centralised platform where staff can manage animal records, update information, organise events, handle visitor bookings, and manage sponsorship programmes. Visitors can use the system to explore animals, learn about the zoo, purchase memberships, make bookings, and support animals through sponsorship. RescueMS aims to make zoo management more efficient while providing visitors with a simple, accessible, and engaging digital experience.

Featured Project

Climate Clarity

Climate Clarity is an AI-powered climate information platform developed to make complex climate reports easier for people in Nepal to understand. The project transforms lengthy and technical climate reports into simple visual summaries, interactive information for all 77 districts, transparency scorecards, bilingual audio explanations, and a promise tracker for monitoring climate commitments. By making climate data more accessible and understandable, Climate Clarity aims to empower citizens to stay informed, understand local climate issues, and take meaningful action towards a more sustainable future.
